Research Areas

My research spans data visualization, NLP, and learning analytics — with a focus on making sense of complex human interactions in educational settings.

🕸️

Network Analysis & Graph Visualization

Developing and applying graph-based methods to visualize semantic relationships, epistemic networks, and social structures in online learning communities.

Network Graphs ENA D3.js
💬

NLP in Educational Discourse

Using large language models, LSTM networks, and semantic analysis to measure discourse coherence, social presence, and cognitive engagement in discussion boards.

NLP LSTM CoI Framework
🤖

Generative AI in Higher Education

Studying student perceptions, performance outcomes, and pedagogical implications of Generative AI tools across disciplines at MSU Denver through SoTL research.

GenAI SoTL Mixed Methods
📊

Machine Learning & Distributed Systems

Exploring scalability and performance of ML workloads on distributed hardware — including Raspberry Pi clusters — to make high-performance computing accessible.

ML HPC Raspberry Pi
